## Body
> Hello, Sharders!
>
> I'm Manu, a Spanish-speaking Cosmere fan who's been learning to code. Over the last months I've been
> building **Trivia del Cosmere**, a trivia game meant for reading groups and friends playing
> together on **one phone**.
>
> **The game.** 1–6 players or teams take turns spinning a wheel of six worlds. Two correct answers
> in a world infuse its gem; infuse all six and you get the final question. Each player has three
> lifelines per game: Atium (removes two wrong answers), Stormlight (lets you try again if you miss)
> and Breath (swaps the question).
>
> **Spoilers.** This was the thing I cared about most. Every question is tagged with the latest book
> you need to answer it, and you tick the books you've read before playing, so you only get
> questions you can actually know.
>
> **Also included:** almost 500 questions in four difficulty levels, a daily challenge (same 5
> questions for everyone that day), a quick game against the clock, and a practice mode with an
> explanation after each answer.
>
> **Languages.** English, Spanish, French, German and Italian, using the names from each language's
> published edition. Some terms in the translations are still being reviewed, so if you read the
> books in one of those languages, corrections are very welcome.
>
> It's free, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking, and it works offline once loaded (install it
> from your browser with "Add to Home Screen"):
> https://manuvp90.github.io/ManuVP90/
>
> To be clear: this is an **unofficial fan project**, not affiliated with or endorsed by Brandon
> Sanderson or Dragonsteel. I built it with help from AI coding assistants, and the questions and
> translations were reviewed and checked against the books. *(Quitar esta frase si el staff pide no
> mencionarlo; mantenerla si dicen que es necesario declararlo.)*
>
> What I'd love from you: questions that are wrong, ambiguous or too easy for their level (each
> question has a "Report a mistake" button), and anything about the rules that's confusing. Thank
> you!
